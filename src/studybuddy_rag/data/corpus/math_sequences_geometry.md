# Arithmetic progressions
An arithmetic progression is a sequence where each term is found by adding the same number, the common difference, to the previous term. In 5, 8, 11, 14, ... the common difference is 3. The n-th term is a + (n - 1)d, where a is the first term and d is the common difference. The sum of the first n terms is n/2 × (2a + (n - 1)d).

# Angles in a triangle
The three interior angles of any triangle add up to 180 degrees. If two angles of a triangle measure 50 and 60 degrees, the third angle is 180 - 50 - 60 = 70 degrees. A right triangle has one angle of exactly 90 degrees.

# The Pythagorean theorem
In a right triangle the side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse. The Pythagorean theorem says the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides: c² = a² + b². A triangle with legs 3 and 4 has a hypotenuse of 5.

# Area and perimeter
Perimeter is the distance around a shape, while area measures the surface it covers. A rectangle of length 6 m and width 4 m has perimeter 2 × (6 + 4) = 20 m and area 6 × 4 = 24 square metres. The area of a triangle is half the base times the height.
