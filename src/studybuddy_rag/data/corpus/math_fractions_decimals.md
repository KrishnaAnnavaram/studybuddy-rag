# What a fraction means
A fraction describes a part of a whole. The bottom number, the denominator, says how many equal parts the whole is split into. The top number, the numerator, says how many of those parts we take. For example, 3/4 of a pizza means the pizza is cut into 4 equal slices and we take 3 of them.

# Equivalent fractions
Two fractions are equivalent when they name the same amount. Multiplying or dividing the numerator and the denominator by the same non-zero number gives an equivalent fraction, so 1/2 = 2/4 = 50/100. To simplify a fraction, divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor. For instance, 12/18 simplifies to 2/3 because both numbers divide by 6.

# Adding and subtracting fractions
Fractions can only be added directly when they share a denominator. To add 1/3 and 1/4, rewrite both with the common denominator 12: 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12. Subtraction works the same way. The least common multiple of the denominators is the smallest common denominator and keeps the numbers small.

# Converting fractions to decimals
To turn a fraction into a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator. So 3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375. Some fractions give repeating decimals: 1/3 = 0.333... with the 3 repeating forever. A decimal such as 2.75 can be written as 275/100, which simplifies to 11/4.

# Percentages
A percentage is a fraction out of 100. To convert a decimal to a percentage, multiply by 100, so 0.375 becomes 37.5%. To find 20% of 45, multiply 45 by 0.20 to get 9.
