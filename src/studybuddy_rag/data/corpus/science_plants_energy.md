# Photosynthesis
Photosynthesis is the process green plants use to make their own food. In the leaves, chlorophyll absorbs light energy from the Sun. The plant uses that energy to combine carbon dioxide from the air with water from the soil, producing glucose and releasing oxygen. The overall word equation is: carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen, in the presence of light and chlorophyll.

# Respiration
Respiration is how living cells release the energy stored in glucose. In aerobic respiration, glucose reacts with oxygen to give carbon dioxide, water and energy. Plants respire all the time, day and night, while photosynthesis only happens when there is light.

# Plant cells
Plant cells have a cell wall made of cellulose that gives them a fixed shape, a large vacuole filled with cell sap, and chloroplasts that contain chlorophyll. Animal cells have none of these three structures, but both kinds of cell have a nucleus, cytoplasm and a cell membrane.

# The water cycle
Water moves continuously between the land, the oceans and the air. The Sun heats water so it evaporates into water vapour. As the vapour rises it cools and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds. When the droplets join and grow heavy they fall as precipitation, such as rain or snow, and flow back to rivers and seas.
